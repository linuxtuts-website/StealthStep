# StealthStep 👣

A minimalist, privacy-first pedometer for Android. Zero ads, zero telemetry, 100% offline.

## The Philosophy
StealthStep tracks your steps, provides local statistics, and maps your routes entirely on your device. Not a single byte of your health data is ever transmitted to a remote server. 

## Key Features
* **Absolute Privacy:** No internet permissions requested. No data collection.
* **Minimalist UI:** Clean, distraction-free interface.
* **Local Storage Only:** Your data never leaves your smartphone.
* **Open Source:** Fully transparent, auditable codebase.
