# Privacy Policy for StealthStep

**Effective Date: September 2026**

The privacy policy for StealthStep is absolute and non-negotiable: **We collect nothing.**

* **No Telemetry:** We do not use analytics or tracking software.
* **No Advertisements:** No third-party ad networks.
* **Local Data Only:** Step counts and routes are stored exclusively in your device's local database.
* **No Network Access:** No personal or health data is transmitted over the internet.
