### Privacy and Data Security
This application is designed with strict privacy principles:
- **100% Offline:** The app does not transmit telemetry, GPS coordinates, or step data to any external servers.
- **Local Storage:** All metrics and session data are stored locally on your device via SQLite.
- **No Accounts:** No registration or third-party accounts are required to operate this software.

Users are solely responsible for securing their physical devices to protect their locally stored data.

### Telemetry Accuracy
Distance calculations and step counting rely on the hardware sensors and GPS modules of the host device. The developers cannot guarantee absolute precision and are not responsible for discrepancies in fitness tracking or navigation.
